# 🚗 Global Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive 9-visualization executive dashboard built in Tableau to analyze global trends, technology adoption, manufacturer performance, and sustainability metrics across the Electric Vehicle (EV) industry.

The dashboard addresses three distinct operational and strategic scenarios:
- **Scenario 1: Market Strategy & Portfolio Optimization** (Emily Johnson — EV Market Strategy Director)
- **Scenario 2: Automotive Business Intelligence & Competitive Landscape** (David Chen — BI Manager)
- **Scenario 3: Sustainable Mobility & Clean Tech Research** (Dr. Sarah Williams — Research Analyst)
---

## 🚀 Key Features & Visualizations

### Scenario 1: Executive Market Strategy
1. **Sales Volume & Total Revenue by Category:** Identifies top-contributing vehicle segments by units and dollar volume.
2. **Top 10 Manufacturers by Sales & Customer Satisfaction:** Ranks top sellers and evaluates customer rating distributions.
3. **Category Driving Range (Km):** Analyzes range performance across vehicle categories.

### Scenario 2: Business Intelligence & Competition
4. **Regional Market Share:** Treemap breakdown of regional market penetration.
5. **Production Origin vs. Destination Region:** Matrix heatmap mapping manufacturing countries to export markets.
6. **Battery Tech Adoption & Price Point:** Dual-axis chart comparing sales volume and average price across battery chemistries.

### Scenario 3: Research & Sustainability
7. **Battery Technology Efficiency Grid:** Scatter matrix analyzing capacity ($kWh$), driving range ($km$), and 5-year degradation.
8. **Annual Environmental Impact (CO2 Saved):** Quantifies carbon reduction (tons/year) by category and chemistry.
9. **Customer Rating vs. 3-Year Resale Value:** Linear trend analysis examining value retention against customer satisfaction.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public:** Dashboard design, calculated fields, and interactive cross-filtering.
- **Data Source:** CSV dataset containing 200,000+ vehicle records (sales, pricing, battery specs, regional metrics).
